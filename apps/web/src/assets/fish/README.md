# Seafood imagery

The six `*-ice.webp` assets are AI-generated illustrations of katsuo, sanma, saba, hotate, mebachi maguro and awabi on crushed ice. They are optimized WebP copies of the generated originals. They are presentation assets, not lot photographs, landing evidence, classifier inputs or validated species references. Submitted landing photos take precedence in live listings and lot details.
